Institutional Profile

Executive Summary & Philosophy

Founded on the principles of rational inquiry, creative dialogue, and civilizational renaissance, the Center serves as a bridge between Iqbal’s timeless wisdom and contemporary society.

Our Mission

To acquaint scholars, students, and the general public with the thought, literature, and philosophy of Allama Iqbal through structured reading circles, university discourse, and creative programming — presenting his immortal literary treasury to a new generation of critical thinkers.

Our Vision: Transformation to AISCC

To transform this platform into the Allama Iqbal Study & Cultural Centre (AISCC), fostering a renaissance of Muslim culture, resisting the counter-culture of hegemonic aggression, and building cultural and linguistic bridges between nations while establishing an international research hub in Dhaka.

Foundational Epistemology

The Five Pillars of Iqbalian Thought

علم

Ilm (Knowledge)

Rigorous academic engagement with canonical texts in Urdu, Persian, Bengali, and English.

عشق

Ishq (Devotion)

A burning, selfless love for truth, cosmic justice, and the upliftment of oppressed humanity.

اجتهاد

Ijtihad (Striving)

Rational, critical, open-minded inquiry — absolute rejection of blind dogmatism and personality cults.

جمعية

Jam'iat (Inclusivity)

Welcoming scholars and youth across every political, religious, and philosophical persuasion.

دوام

Dawam (Continuity)

Sustaining civilizational legacy through institutional archives, scholarships, and youth custody.

Academic Output

Publications, Translation & Qawwali Academy

Transforming Iqbal's poetry of Khudi into living artistic expressions: quarterly research journals, translation annotations, classical Sufi Qawwali, and youth recitations.

Quarterly Research Journal

Iqbaaliyaat (ইকবালিয়াাত)

Peer-reviewed academic quarterly journal printing 500 copies per issue. Featuring critical essays, unpublished translations, and thesis chapters on Iqbalian metaphysics and geopolitics.

500 Copies / Issue National Distribution
Foundational Translation

Urdu & Persian to Bengali

Direct manuscript translations of Shikwa, Jawab-e-Shikwa, Asrar-i-Khudi, and selected ghazals with verse-by-verse Bengali commentary for students and literary researchers.

Direct Manuscripts Bengali & English
Acoustic & Fine Arts Wing

Qawwali & Drama Academy
